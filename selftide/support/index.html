Common questions

Where is my data kept? Is it backed up anywhere?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. Selftide has no account, no server, and no cloud sync — so there is no copy for us to look at, and none for us to restore.

That means backups are yours to keep. Use Settings → Your data → Export to save a file somewhere safe. If you delete the app without exporting, the data is gone, and we cannot get it back for you.

How do I get my data out?

Settings → Your data → Export. It produces one JSON file containing your fasts, drinks, body measurements, any workout and sleep records imported from Apple Health, your settings, and the record of which disclaimers you accepted.

It is free, it includes everything, and it always will.

How do I get it back in?

Settings → Your data → Restore, and pick a file you exported. Your history is added to whatever is already there and nothing is deleted, so restoring twice does not duplicate anything. Your settings are replaced by the ones in the file.

I connected Apple Health but my weight isn't appearing

This one is a genuine iOS limitation rather than a bug, and it catches everybody.

**iOS never tells an app whether permission to read was granted.** Selftide asks, and from then on either data arrives or it doesn't — the app cannot tell the difference between "you said no" and "there is nothing there".

Check the read toggles yourself: Health app → your profile → Apps and Services → Selftide → Data Access & Devices. Make sure the categories you want are switched on for reading.

It is also why the Settings screen says "Requested" rather than "Connected" for sleep and workouts — saying "Connected" would be claiming something we cannot actually know.

Sleep and workouts are connected, but nothing shows them

That is expected today. Those are collected so that the insights built on them later have history to work from. Connecting is entirely optional and everything else works without it.

The lock screen timer disappeared partway through a fast

iOS ends Live Activities after about eight hours. Nothing is lost — the fast is still running and the time is still correct; only the lock screen display stops.

Turn on Settings → Notifications → Keep lock screen timer alive and the last reminder invites you to open the app, which starts a fresh one.

My reminders aren't arriving

Three things to check, in order:

1. iOS Settings → Selftide → Notifications — allow them. 2. Selftide → Settings → Notifications — the specific reminder is on. 3. Quiet hours, in the same section. Alerts that mark a moment — a goal reached, a stage crossed — are skipped inside that window rather than arriving late.

A scheduled-fast reminder also needs the schedule itself switched on, with at least one day selected.

Does Selftide start and end my fasts for me?

No, and it will not. A schedule reminds you and can count down to the next fast on the lock screen, but every fast starts and ends because you said so. A fast the app invented would corrupt every streak and statistic derived from it.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. That removes everything it stored on this device, with no copy on a server to ask about afterwards.

Weight readings Selftide wrote into Apple Health belong to Apple Health and stay there — delete those in the Health app.

Why can't I use the fasting features?

Selftide is for adults, and some answers in the setup health screening switch the fasting features off rather than showing a warning — pregnancy, a history of disordered eating, and some medical conditions among them.

That is deliberate, and we would rather be too careful here than not careful enough. If fasting is something you are considering, talk to a healthcare professional about it.

What we can't help with

  • Recovering data from a deleted app. There is no copy. This is the cost

of the app not collecting anything, and we would rather be straight about it than pretend otherwise.

  • Medical advice of any kind. Selftide is an educational tracking tool, not

a medical device, and we are not clinicians. Whether fasting is safe for you is a question for a qualified healthcare professional.

  • Anything urgent about your health. Selftide is not monitored and nobody

sees what you record in it. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
