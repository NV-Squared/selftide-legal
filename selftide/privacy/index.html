Selftide Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 Last updated: 17 October 2026

Selftide is made by NV Squared LLC. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

It is short because the app does very little with it.

The short version

Selftide does not collect your data. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything you record stays on your iPhone.

The app contains no networking code at all — it cannot send your information anywhere, because it has no way to make a network request. That isn't a policy promise about what we choose not to do; it's a property of how the app is built.

The only time your data leaves the device is when you export it.

What Selftide stores

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your fasts — when each one started and ended, which plan it followed, whether you met your goal, the time zone you were in, and any notes you add
  • Drinks you log — water, coffee, tea, electrolytes, and when
  • Body measurements — weight and, if you connect Apple Health, other body metrics
  • Your settings — units, notification preferences, quiet hours, your fasting schedule, and a weight goal if you set one
  • Your acknowledgement of the health disclaimer — which version you accepted and the date. Kept so we both have a record of what you were told

There is no account and no sign-in. Selftide never asks for your name, email address, or phone number, and it does not generate an advertising or tracking identifier for you.

Widgets and the lock screen

If you add a Selftide widget, or while a fast is running with the Live Activity showing, three pieces of information are copied into a private storage area shared between the app and its widget — when your fast started, when it is due to end, and the name of the plan you are following. If you have set a fasting schedule and left its Countdown to next fast switch on, the start and goal times of your next week of planned fasts are copied there too, so the widget and the lock screen can count down to the next one between fasts; with that switch off, nothing about your schedule is copied. Nothing else is shared: not your weight, not your Apple Health data, not your history.

This area is still on your device and still inside Selftide's own sandbox. Other apps cannot read it, and it is removed when you delete Selftide.

One thing worth knowing: widgets and Live Activities are designed to be readable without unlocking your phone. Anyone who can see your screen can see that you are fasting and for how long, or when your next fast is planned. If you would rather not show that, remove the Selftide widget from your home and lock screens, and turn off Live Activities for Selftide in iOS Settings → Selftide. The countdown has its own switch in Selftide's Settings, under Schedule, so you can keep the schedule's reminders without it.

What Selftide does not do

  • No servers. There is no Selftide backend. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs. We do not measure how you use the app.
  • No advertising, no trackers, no third-party code. The app has zero third-party dependencies — it is built from our own code and Apple's frameworks only.
  • No selling or sharing of your data. There is no one to share it with.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Selftide asks your permission before reading anything, and you can refuse or change your mind at any time in the Health app under Data Access & Devices.

What it reads: only what the app actually uses, which today is your weight. Health data read into Selftide is copied into the app's local storage so your charts work offline. It is not transmitted anywhere. Body fat percentage, lean body mass and blood glucose are planned, and Selftide will ask for them when the features that show them exist — not before.

What it writes: weight only. When you log a weight in Selftide, it can save that reading back to Apple Health so your records live in one place. Selftide writes nothing else — in particular it does not write your fasts to Apple Health.

Sleep, resting heart rate and workouts are a separate, optional permission, offered under Settings → Apple Health → Connect sleep & workouts. Two things are worth being plain about: nothing in the app displays them yet, and the reason to collect them now is that the insights built on them later need history to already exist. Connecting is entirely optional, everything else works without it, and refusing does not affect your weight connection.

Each category is requested on its own, and you can change any of them at any time in the Health app.

Health data obtained through Apple Health is used only to provide features inside the app. It is never used for advertising or marketing, and it is never shared with third parties.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. There is no push server, and no notification content leaves your phone.

Exporting your data

Selftide can export everything it holds as a JSON file, from Settings → Your data. This only happens when you ask for it.

The file contains your fasts, drinks, body measurements, and any workout or sleep records imported from Apple Health. It also contains your settings — units, display density, notification preferences, quiet hours, your fasting schedule, and the plan you follow — so that reinstalling the app does not mean setting it up again from scratch. And it records which version of the health disclaimer you accepted and when, so that you hold your own copy of it.

Your health screening answers are not exported. What you told us during onboarding about pregnancy, eating-disorder history, diabetes or medication stays on the device and is never written to a file. Reinstalling asks again.

Once you save or send that file, it is outside the app's control — it lives wherever you put it, under whatever protections that place provides. It contains your health information, so treat it accordingly.

You can import an export back into Selftide on any device. Your history is added to whatever is already there and nothing is deleted; your settings replace the ones currently in the app. Importing never changes your recorded acceptance of the disclaimer — if the wording has changed since, you are asked again rather than a file being allowed to answer for you.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. There is no copy on a server to request removal of, because there is no server.

Data that Selftide wrote into Apple Health (weight readings) belongs to Apple Health and stays there; you can delete it in the Health app.

Your rights

Because your information never leaves your device, the usual data rights are things you exercise directly rather than by asking us:

  • Access and portability — export your data at any time from Settings
  • Erasure — delete the app, or delete individual fasts from the History screen
  • Correction — edit any fast's start and end times in the app

If you are in the UK, EU, California, or another region with data protection laws, this remains true: we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete on your behalf.

Children

Selftide is not intended for anyone under 18, and its onboarding declines to enable fasting features for under-18 users. We do not knowingly collect information from children — and as described above, we do not collect information from anyone.

Health disclaimer

Selftide is an educational and tracking tool. It is not medical advice. Metabolic stage indicators are estimates that vary considerably between individuals. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before changing how you eat, particularly if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a history of disordered eating, have diabetes, or take medication affecting blood sugar.

If NV Squared is ever sold

If NV Squared LLC is acquired, merges, or sells the app, your information on your device stays where it is — on your device. Any future operator of Selftide would take it on subject to the commitments in this policy, including the ones Apple's rules make permanent: Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never mined, and never sold.

We would tell you in the app before any change of ownership altered how your information is handled, and you would have the choice described in the next section.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date, and material changes will be noted in the app.

A new use of your information is not something a policy update can decide for you. If Selftide ever wants to do something with your data that this policy does not already describe — sync it to the cloud, measure how the app is used, send you marketing, or anything else — we will ask you, in the app, before it happens, for that specific purpose. You can say no and carry on using the app, and you can change your mind later.

Two things will not change, because they are not ours to change: data read from Apple Health is used only to provide features inside the app and is never used for advertising or marketing, never mined, and never sold to anyone — and your health screening answers are never exported or shared.
